# Compare consumption with the agreement's estimates

The cost-sharing agreement of 5 Sep 2026 gives expected consumption for the shared utilities.
Readings (kWh, m³) are saved with each bill and charted per bill on the Reports page, but nothing
compares them with those estimates.

- Store an estimate per service and period (likely on the service: expected kWh or m³ per month,
  or per billing period), entered by an admin.
- On Reports, draw the estimate against the readings, and flag a period well above it.
- Decide whether a reading far above the estimate should also notify, like a price change.
- The plan named uPlot for these charts; the current bars may be enough once an estimate line is
  added.
